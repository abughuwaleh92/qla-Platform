Did you know?

Between any two rational numbers, there are infinitely many other rational numbers! This property is called "density" of rational numbers.

Convert Repeating Decimals to Fractions

Example: Convert 0.333... to a fraction

Let x = 0.333...

Then 10x = 3.333...

10x - x = 3.333... - 0.333...

9x = 3

x = 3/9 = 1/3
